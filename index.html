About the Studio

We Create
Spaces
with Purpose

"Where aesthetics, functionality, and harmony meet."

Founded with a passion for spatial storytelling, Sakarya Space has grown into an architecture studio that stands as the right choice for bringing your dream spaces to life. Our approach blends Scandinavian precision with Japanese spatial philosophy — creating spaces that breathe, inspire, and endure across time.

Every project is a collaboration — a dialogue between client vision and our design expertise. We believe the best architecture is not seen but felt, in the way light moves through a room, how space responds to human presence.
